Everything you need, nothing you don't

A focused feature set built around one goal: helping you work in short, sustainable bursts — without selling your attention to anyone else.

⏱️

Customizable timer

Set your own focus, short-break, and long-break lengths, plus how many focus sessions happen before a long break.

🔔

Optional sound alerts

Get a gentle tone when a session ends, and an optional heads-up warning before time runs out.

🚧

Focus-session site blocking

Blacklist distracting sites, or lock in with a whitelist of only what you need. Blocking is active during focus time only — breaks are always unrestricted.

🔒

100% local

No accounts, no servers, no analytics. Your settings, timer state, and focus-time history live in your browser's local storage and never leave your device.

🚫

No ads, no paywall

Every feature is free and available from the moment you install it. Nothing to unlock.

📖

Open source

The full source is public on GitHub, so you — or anyone — can see exactly what it does.

How it works

The classic Pomodoro technique, tuned to your pace.

Start a focus session

Click the OpenTomato icon and hit Start. Default is 25 minutes — fully adjustable in settings.

Take short breaks

After each focus session, a short break begins automatically — no blocking, no restrictions.

Recharge with a long break

After four focus sessions (or however many you choose), enjoy a longer break before starting over.

Try the browser demo

A no-install taste of the timer, alert sounds, and notifications, running right in your browser. Tasks, site blocking, Restrictive Mode, and focus stats live in the extension — that's the full version.
