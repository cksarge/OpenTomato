Timer

Restrictive Mode

When restrictive mode is on, starting a focus session locks things down until that session ends:

  • Timer durations and every site-blocking setting are frozen.
  • The timer can't be skipped — at all.
  • The timer can only be reset while it's paused, and only after typing a confirmation phrase.
  • Blocked pages lose their “Continue anyway” button.
